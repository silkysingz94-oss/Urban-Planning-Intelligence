5. UP & UD Agentic AI

A conceptual multi-agent workflow for urban planning and urban design intelligence. The agents describe proposed capabilities rather than autonomous services operating in this page.

Data Acquisition Agent

Identifies available geospatial sources, retrieves open data and checks coverage, date and completeness.

Spatial Analysis Agent

Supports building-footprint analysis, spatial overlays, morphology measures and geospatial summaries.

Planning Intelligence Agent

Translates spatial evidence into planning questions, options and decision-support outputs.

6. Generative AI

A conceptual four-phase framework for AI-assisted urban planning and design.

Phase 1 · Theoretical Grounding & Urban Context

Establish planning context, urban morphology, spatial constraints, data provenance and relevant design principles.

Phase 2 · Plan Compilation

Assemble base maps, street networks, building footprints, land-use information and development parameters.

Phase 3 · Evaluation

Assess spatial structure, accessibility, land-use compatibility, built-form characteristics and scenario trade-offs.

Phase 4 · Validation

Validate outputs against authoritative data, local knowledge, field evidence, planning standards and stakeholder review.

Data limitations

OpenStreetMap building footprints are volunteered geographic information. Missing mapped buildings do not imply that buildings are absent on the ground. Footprint area is not floor area, occupancy, population or building height. Planning decisions require validation using authoritative data and local evidence.
